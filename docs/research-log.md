# Research log

This log keeps what the project owners decided apart from what still has to be found out. Entries are dated and are not rewritten afterwards. When a record is replaced, the old entry stays and points to the newer one.

A technical specification shows a proposed design. It is not evidence of demand, of working software or of funding.
