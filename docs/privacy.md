# Privacy and data protection

This page covers how the project handles data in this repository and in its research, and what an association would have to decide before running the register. No register is running anywhere yet.

## In this repository

- Examples use fictional records only.
- Names of associations, quotes and letters appear only with permission for the intended audience.
- Raw research responses, contact details and signed letters are never added.

## In the research

- Contact details are kept separately from responses.
- Access to raw responses is restricted.
- Raw data has a set deletion date.

The person responsible for data protection and the retention period have not been decided yet. See the open points in [Research and evidence](research/README.md).

## When an association runs the register

Each association that runs the register has to decide and write down:

- who operates it
- who has which access role, as described in [Architecture](architecture.md)
- how long records are kept
- how backups are made and where they are kept
- whom to contact to have a record corrected or deleted

Information about who controls a service can itself be sensitive, even though the register stores no credentials.
